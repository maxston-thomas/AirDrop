2. Authorized Device Access

Only pair devices that you own or have explicit permission to connect to. The host operator maintains full authority to approve or reject pairing requests and disconnect any client session at any time.

3. Content Ownership and Responsibility

You retain full copyright and ownership of all transferred files. You are solely responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to transfer the files you select and that your files do not violate any applicable intellectual property or privacy laws.

4. Disclaimers and Limitations of Liability

AirLink is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. While cryptographic SHA-256 integrity verification is performed to detect transmission errors, the developers cannot guarantee uninterrupted wireless transfer conditions over fluctuating radio environments.

5. Network Administration and Firewall

Users must configure local firewall permissions only for trusted private networks. Operating AirLink on public or untrusted Wi-Fi networks without an isolated hotspot is discouraged.
