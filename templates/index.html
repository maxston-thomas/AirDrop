    AirDrop Setup & Troubleshooting

    1. Connecting Without Internet Access

    Turn on Mobile Hotspot in Windows Settings (Network & internet → Mobile hotspot). Connect your mobile phone to this Wi-Fi hotspot. Transfers stream at full local Wi-Fi speeds even if Windows displays "No Internet".

    2. Mobile Phone Data Routing

    When connected to offline Wi-Fi, some phones attempt to route browser traffic through Mobile Cellular Data. Temporarily turn off Mobile Data on your phone, or tap the system alert and select "Stay Connected".

    3. Full Quality File Integrity

    Unlike messaging platforms, AirDrop never resizes images or recompresses 4K videos. The cryptographic SHA-256 hash mathematically verifies that every byte matches the original file.
